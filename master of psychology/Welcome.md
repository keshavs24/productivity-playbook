# Master of Psychology

A structured knowledge vault for mastering psychology, persuasion, negotiation, and communication. Part of the Productivity Playbook.

## How This Works
- **raw/** — Source documents organized by curriculum phase. Immutable.
- **wiki/** — LLM-generated knowledge pages. Summaries, concepts, entities, comparisons, applications.
- **index.md** — Catalog of everything in the wiki.
- **log.md** — Chronological record of all operations.

## The Curriculum
1. Cognitive Foundations
2. Decision Science
3. Motivation Science
4. Communication Foundations
5. Negotiation
6. Advanced Persuasion
7. Leadership Communication

## Getting Started
Drop source documents into the appropriate `raw/phase-N/` folder and tell the LLM to ingest.
