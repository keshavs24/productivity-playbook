# What-So What-Now What

**Phase(s):** 7
**Confidence:** 0.7

## Definition

What-So What-Now What is a three-part communication structure described by Matt Abrahams as the "Swiss Army knife" of spontaneous speaking frameworks. It organizes any message into three sequential components: (1) **What** -- the topic, idea, product, or argument; (2) **So What** -- why it matters, why it's important, helpful, or relevant; (3) **Now What** -- what the audience should do with this knowledge, the practical implications or next steps.

## Key Findings

- Abrahams considers this "my favorite structure of all time" due to its simplicity and versatility ([[summary-7-26-tfts-ch5]])
- The structure works for virtually any spontaneous speaking situation: presentations, Q&A, feedback, small talk, toasts, and pitches ([[summary-7-26-tfts-ch5]], [[summary-7-26-tfts-app1]])
- Sarah Zeitler used What-So What-Now What to deliver a colleague's entire product launch presentation with no preparation -- and was praised by senior leadership ([[summary-7-26-tfts-ch5]])
- In small talk, the "Now What" naturally becomes a question that invites the other person to contribute, creating collaborative conversational flow ([[summary-7-26-tfts-app1]])

## Mechanisms

The structure works because it maps to how the human brain naturally processes and evaluates information:

1. **What** addresses the factual question: "What are you talking about?" -- it orients the listener
2. **So What** addresses the evaluative question: "Why should I care?" -- it establishes relevance and stakes
3. **Now What** addresses the action question: "What do I do with this?" -- it provides closure and next steps

By answering these three questions in sequence, the speaker creates a logical narrative arc (beginning, middle, end) with built-in transitions. The structure also prevents common pitfalls: rambling (What constrains scope), irrelevance (So What forces audience-centricity), and trailing off without a point (Now What provides closure).

## Applications

### Small Talk (Application #1)
- **Initiating:** "So, what did you think of the keynote speaker this morning? [What] How do you think the speaker's ideas will help in the short term? [So What] Do you plan to go to the meet-and-greet the speaker is holding later? [Now What]"
- **Responding:** "Well, I was originally born in Omaha, but I moved southward until settling in Houston [What]. While my move was for work, I have really enjoyed the opportunity to attend lots of sporting events and eat really good food [So What]. So, I am curious to know, have you ever lived in or visited Texas [Now What]?"

### Impromptu Presentations
- Sarah Zeitler's product launch: provided basic information about the product [What], explained why its features mattered [So What], described what needed to happen for a successful launch [Now What]

### General Use
- After reading a news article: What was it about? Why does it matter? What should I do with this information?
- In meetings: state the update [What], explain its significance [So What], propose next steps [Now What]
- Practice by composing mini-presentations in your mind using this formula whenever you consume information
