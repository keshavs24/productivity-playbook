# Pre-Suasion

**Phase(s):** 6 — Advanced Persuasion
**Confidence:** 0.7

## Definition

Pre-suasion is the process of arranging for recipients to be receptive to a message *before* they encounter it. Rather than focusing on the content of a persuasive appeal, pre-suasion focuses on what a communicator says, does, or arranges *immediately prior* to delivering that appeal. The core insight: the best persuaders spend more time cultivating the psychological ground than perfecting the seed.

The term was coined by [[cialdini]] to describe a class of influence techniques that operate by strategically directing preliminary attention to concepts that are associatively aligned with the forthcoming message. The techniques work through two mechanisms: (1) elevating the importance and [[focal-attention-is-causal|perceived causality]] of whatever is made focal, and (2) activating associations in the audience's mind that are favorable to the communicator's goal.

## Key Findings

- **Top performers pre-suade.** Across sales, fundraising, advertising, PR, and corporate recruiting, the highest-performing influence professionals were distinguished not by their messages but by what they did *before* delivering them (Cialdini's three-year participant observation study)
- **The effect is immediate and temporal.** Pre-suasive practices create [[privileged-moments]] — windows of opportunity that are far from propped open permanently. The receptivity they generate is only temporary, which is why timing is critical.
- **It operates outside awareness.** Participants in Mandel and Johnson's furniture study, Bolkan and Andersen's survey study, and numerous other experiments denied that pre-suasive openers influenced their decisions
- **It operates from infancy.** 18-month-old infants exposed to togetherness images became three times more helpful, spontaneously and without prompting
- **It is constrained by associative distance.** The strength of a pre-suasive effect depends on the strength of the association between the opener and the desired response (Cialdini's littering study showed a clear gradient)

## Mechanisms

Pre-suasion works through **cognitive accessibility**: when an opener concept receives attention, closely linked secondary concepts become more accessible in consciousness (readied for action), while unlinked concepts get suppressed (temporarily decommissioned). This dual process — activation plus suppression — explains the power of the technique.

Three channels of pre-suasion:
1. **Attention → Importance**: Whatever receives focused attention is perceived as more important ([[focusing-illusion]])
2. **Attention → Causality**: Whatever is focal is perceived as causal ([[focal-attention-is-causal]])
3. **Attention → Association**: Whatever concept is activated spreads to linked concepts via [[associative-priming]]

## Applications

- **Sales**: Establish trust pre-suasively before presenting (Jim the fire alarm salesman)
- **Pricing**: Mention an absurdly high number before stating the actual price (the "$1 million" consulting tactic)
- **Marketing**: Background cues on websites (fluffy clouds vs. pennies) shift consumer priorities without awareness
- **Health communication**: Pair frightening health facts with actionable steps to prevent denial
- **Self-influence**: Use [[if-then-plans]] to install pre-suasive cue-action associations for personal goals
- **Writing/Creativity**: Choose work environments whose cues match the desired output style
- **Education**: Begin lectures with mystery stories to magnetize attention and deepen learning
- **Negotiation**: Use language that channels associations toward concepts favorable to your position

## Open Questions

- How long do pre-suasive effects persist? Most evidence shows temporary windows, but some conditioning studies show effects growing over weeks
- What is the interaction between pre-suasion and the strength of the subsequent argument? The evidence suggests pre-suasion amplifies strong arguments but can backfire with weak ones
- Can people develop stable resistance to pre-suasive techniques? The corrective evidence suggests awareness can neutralize effects, but modern life's pace makes sustained awareness difficult
