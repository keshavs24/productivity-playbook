# Phase 6: Advanced Persuasion

## Purpose

Phase 6 deepens the influence foundations of Phase 1 into advanced persuasion science. Where Phase 1 introduced Cialdini's six principles as defensive tools, Phase 6 explores the offensive architecture of influence: what happens *before* the message (pre-suasion), how ideas spread virally (contagion), and how persuasion techniques are weaponized at scale (propaganda). The central question: how can the full toolkit of psychological influence be deployed ethically and effectively — and how can you defend against its misuse?

## Phase 6 Synthesis

Phase 6 completes the persuasion arc that began with Aristotle (Phase 4) and was grounded in Cialdini's six principles (Phase 1). Pre-Suasion reveals that the most powerful moment for influence is *before* the message — what you draw attention to, what associations you activate, what environment you create. Berger shows how ideas spread through social networks via six STEPPS principles that complement Heath's SUCCESs framework. Pratkanis and Aronson provide the critical/ethical lens: understanding how these same techniques are weaponized as propaganda, and how inoculation theory can build resistance.

The convergence across phases is striking: pre-suasion (Cialdini) = privileged moments for framing (Kahneman) = the opening move before negotiation (Voss/Fisher) = the "before" in communication strategy (Munter). The curriculum now provides a complete toolkit from cognitive foundations through applied influence.
