# Pre-Suasion: A Revolutionary Way to Influence and Persuade

**Type:** Book
**Phase(s):** 6 — Advanced Persuasion
**Key Contribution:** Establishes the science of what communicators do *before* delivering a message to make audiences receptive — extending Cialdini's earlier work on the six principles of influence from *what* works to *when* and *how to prepare* for it

## Overview

Published by Simon & Schuster in 2016, *Pre-Suasion* is [[cialdini]]'s follow-up to *[[influence-science-and-practice|Influence]]*, extending his influence framework from the content of persuasive messages to their *timing and preparation*. Where *Influence* identified six universal principles that make messages effective, *Pre-Suasion* asks: what can a communicator do in the moment *before* delivering a message to make audiences maximally receptive?

The book's central thesis: the best persuaders become the best not through the quality of their arguments but through the process of [[pre-suasion]] — arranging for recipients to be receptive to a message before they encounter it. Three key mechanisms explain why this works: (1) whatever receives focused attention is perceived as more important ([[focusing-illusion]]), (2) whatever is focal is perceived as causal ([[focal-attention-is-causal]]), and (3) attention to one concept activates associated concepts while suppressing unassociated ones ([[associative-priming]]).

The book draws on Cialdini's continued methodology of combining rigorous experimental evidence with real-world observation among influence professionals, and represents a shift in emphasis from *defensive* knowledge (how to resist influence, the focus of *Influence*) to *offensive* knowledge (how to become more influential).

## Key Ideas

- **Pre-suasion** — What communicators do *before* delivering a message to make audiences receptive
- **Privileged moments** — Temporary windows of heightened receptivity following pre-suasive openers
- **Attention = Importance = Causality** — The linked chain that makes channeled attention so powerful
- **Commanders of attention** — Attractors (sexual, threatening, different) and magnetizers (self-relevant, unfinished, mysterious)
- **Associations as building blocks** — All mental activity arises from associations; influence works by managing which associations are activated
- **Persuasive geography** — Physical and psychological environments shape behavior through their associative cues
- **If/when-then plans** — The most powerful form of self-directed pre-suasion
- **Corrective mechanisms** — Awareness of bias sources can neutralize pre-suasive effects, but modern life increasingly deprives people of the cognitive resources needed for correction
