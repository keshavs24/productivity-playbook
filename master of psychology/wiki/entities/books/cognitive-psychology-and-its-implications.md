# Cognitive Psychology and Its Implications

**Type:** Book (9th Edition)
**Phase(s):** 2
**Author:** John R. Anderson
**Key Contribution:** Comprehensive cognitive science textbook providing mechanistic accounts of problem-solving, expertise, reasoning, and decision-making

## Overview

Anderson's textbook is one of the definitive introductions to cognitive psychology. For this curriculum, four chapters (8-11) were selected to provide the cognitive science foundations that complement the behavioral economics perspective of Kahneman/Tversky and the managerial decision science of Bazerman/Moore.

Where Kahneman documents systematic biases and Bazerman applies them to management, Anderson explains the underlying cognitive architecture — how problems are represented in memory, how expertise develops through chunking and proceduralization, how reasoning departs from formal logic, and how decision-making integrates emotion and cognition.

## Key Ideas

### Problem Solving (Ch.8)
- [[problem-space]] — Initial state, goal state, operators framework
- [[means-ends-analysis]] — Reduce largest difference between current and goal
- [[difference-reduction]] — Hill-climbing heuristic
- [[functional-fixedness]] — Conventional-use bias blocks creative solutions
- [[set-effect]] / [[einstellung-effect]] — Prior solutions block better alternatives
- [[incubation-effect]] — Stepping away dissipates set effects

### Expertise (Ch.9)
- [[deliberate-practice]] — 10,000+ hours of structured, feedback-rich practice
- [[chunking]] — Expert pattern recognition from meaningful grouping
- [[expert-intuition]] — Rapid, accurate System 1 judgment from extensive practice
- [[proceduralization]] — Declarative → procedural knowledge conversion

### Reasoning (Ch.10)
- [[categorical-syllogisms]] — Errors in logical inference from quantified premises
- [[conditional-reasoning]] — If-then logic including Wason selection task
- [[wason-selection-task]] — Exposes confirmation bias in abstract reasoning
- [[permission-schema]] — Social-contract framing improves logical performance
- [[mental-model-theory]] — Reasoning via internal simulations, not formal rules

### Decision Making (Ch.11)
- [[bayesian-conservatism]] — Under-updating relative to Bayes' theorem
- [[iowa-gambling-task]] — Somatic markers guide advantageous decisions
- [[recognition-heuristic]] — Recognized option inferred as higher value
- Convergence with [[prospect-theory]], [[loss-aversion]], [[framing-effects]]
