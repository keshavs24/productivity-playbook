# Age of Propaganda: The Everyday Use and Abuse of Persuasion

**Type:** Book
**Phase(s):** 6 — Advanced Persuasion
**Key Contribution:** Comprehensive analysis of how propaganda and persuasion techniques operate in everyday life, organized around four stratagems of influence (pre-persuasion, source credibility, message, emotions) with practical defenses including inoculation theory.

## Overview

Published by W. H. Freeman (Revised Edition, 2001), *Age of Propaganda* by Anthony Pratkanis and Elliot Aronson is a survey of the social psychology of persuasion applied to advertising, politics, cults, media, and daily life. The book argues that modern democracy faces a fundamental dilemma: citizens are cognitive misers operating in a message-dense, persuasion-saturated environment that promotes mindless, peripheral processing — the hallmark of propaganda rather than deliberative persuasion.

The book's central framework organizes persuasion around four stratagems that propagandists must accomplish for maximum influence: (1) [[pre-persuasion-tactics]] — define the situation favorably; (2) establish [[source-credibility]]; (3) construct a focused message; (4) control the target's emotions. These map to the classical rhetorical tradition of Aristotle ([[ethos]], [[logos]], [[pathos]]) plus Cicero's concept of *statis*.

The book draws on the [[elaboration-likelihood-model]] (Petty & Cacioppo) to distinguish propaganda (peripheral-route processing) from genuine persuasion (central-route processing), and concludes with defenses including [[inoculation-theory]] (McGuire), [[stealing-thunder]], and institutional reforms.

## Key Concepts Introduced or Developed

- [[pre-persuasion-tactics]] — The first stratagem; defining the situation before arguments begin
- [[granfalloon]] — Meaningless group identity exploited for influence (term from Vonnegut; science from Tajfel)
- [[phantom-alternative]] — Unavailable options that shift preferences
- [[fear-appeal]] — The four conditions for effective fear-based persuasion
- [[mindlessness-in-persuasion]] — Langer's research applied to propaganda
- [[inoculation-theory]] — Building resistance through pre-exposure to weak arguments
- [[propaganda-vs-persuasion]] — The ethical distinction based on thought-induction vs. thought-truncation
- [[elaboration-likelihood-model]] — Applied as the diagnostic framework for propaganda detection
