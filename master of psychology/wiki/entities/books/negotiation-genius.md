# Negotiation Genius

**Type:** Book
**Phase(s):** 5
**Key Contribution:** Comprehensive negotiation framework integrating value claiming, value creation, investigative negotiation, psychological influence, and strategies for weakness — the practical complement to [[getting-to-yes]]

## Overview

*Negotiation Genius: How to Overcome Obstacles and Achieve Brilliant Results at the Bargaining Table and Beyond* (2007) by [[malhotra|Deepak Malhotra]] and [[bazerman|Max Bazerman]] is a Harvard Business School-based negotiation text that bridges academic research and practical dealmaking. The book is organized in three parts:

- **Part I: The Negotiator's Toolkit** — claiming value (Ch. 1), creating value (Ch. 2), and [[investigative-negotiation]] (Ch. 3)
- **Part II: The Psychology of Negotiation** — cognitive biases (Ch. 4), motivational biases (Ch. 5), and strategies for overcoming and leveraging biases (Ch. 6)
- **Part III: Negotiating in the Real World** — [[influence-strategies-in-negotiation|influence strategies]] (Ch. 7), blind spots (Ch. 8), lies and deception (Ch. 9), ethics (Ch. 10), [[negotiating-from-weakness|weakness]] (Ch. 11), ugly negotiations (Ch. 12), when not to negotiate (Ch. 13), and the path to genius (Ch. 14)

The book's distinctive contribution relative to other negotiation texts is its integration of behavioral decision research into practical strategy. While [[getting-to-yes]] established the principled negotiation framework, *Negotiation Genius* provides the tactical, operational detail for *executing* negotiations — including what to do when the other side does not cooperate, when you have no power, and when influence tactics are being used against you.

## Key Ideas

- **Five-step preparation framework:** Assess your [[batna]], calculate your [[reservation-value]], assess the other party's BATNA, calculate their RV, evaluate the [[zopa]] ([[summary-5-17-ng-ch1-claiming-value]])
- **Value creation through logrolling, adding issues, and contingency contracts** ([[summary-5-17-ng-ch2-creating-value]])
- **[[investigative-negotiation]]:** Seven principles and five strategies for uncovering information ([[summary-5-17-ng-ch3-investigative]])
- **Eight influence strategies and six defense strategies** ([[summary-5-17-ng-ch7-influence]])
- **[[negotiating-from-weakness]]:** Five strategies for negotiating without power + three strategies for shifting the balance of power ([[summary-5-17-ng-ch11-weakness]])
- **The [[claiming-vs-creating-value|negotiator's dilemma]]:** The tension between competitive claiming and cooperative creating

## Connections
- **Builds on:** [[getting-to-yes]] (Fisher, Ury, Patton — principled negotiation), [[judgment-in-managerial-decision-making]] (Bazerman & Moore — behavioral decision biases), [[influence-science-and-practice]] (Cialdini — influence principles)
- **Extended by:** Malhotra's later work on trust, deception, and competitive arousal
- **Contradicts:** Pure positional bargaining; the assumption that negotiation is "all art and no science"
- **Applied in:** MBA and executive education at Harvard Business School; consulting with Fortune 500 companies across 25+ countries
