# Think Faster, Talk Smarter

**Type:** Book
**Phase(s):** 7 — Leadership Communication
**Key Contribution:** A comprehensive, evidence-based methodology for improving spontaneous communication through six steps (Calm, Unlock, Redefine, Listen, Structure, Focus) plus six context-specific application structures

## Overview

Published by Macmillan Business in 2023, *Think Faster, Talk Smarter: How to Speak Successfully When You're Put on the Spot* is Matt Abrahams' synthesis of over two decades of teaching, coaching, and researching spontaneous communication at Stanford University. The book addresses the gap between the abundant resources available for formal/prepared speaking and the near-total neglect of impromptu communication -- despite the fact that spontaneous speaking constitutes the vast majority of our daily interactions.

The book's central thesis: spontaneous communication is a learnable skill, not an innate talent. Through a combination of anxiety management, mindset shifts, deep listening, internalized communication structures, and message focus, anyone can become more comfortable, confident, and compelling when put on the spot. Abrahams draws on communication science, cognitive psychology, improvisation, evolutionary biology, and extensive interviews with practitioners across fields.

## Structure

The book is organized into two parts plus appendices:

### Part I: The Think Faster, Talk Smarter Methodology — Six Steps

| Chapter | Step | Title | Core Idea |
|---------|------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Calm | Tame the Anxiety Beast | [[anxiety-management-in-speaking]] -- ABCs of anxiety, AMP framework |
| 2 | Unlock | Maximize Mediocrity | Release perfectionism, hack heuristics, "dare to be dull" |
| 3 | Redefine | Mind Your Mindset | Four mindset shifts: growth, audience-focus, [[yes-and-technique]], [[next-play-mentality]] |
| 4 | Listen | Don't Just Do Something... Stand There! | [[pace-space-grace]] framework, internal/external dialogue |
| 5 | Structure | Structure Your Spontaneity | [[what-so-what-now-what]], [[problem-solution-benefit]], PREP, CCC, STAR |
| 6 | Focus | The F-Word of Spontaneous Speaking | Four dimensions: precision, relevance, accessibility, concision |

### Part II: Talking Smarter in Specific Situations — Six Applications

| Application | Situation | Structure | Key Principle |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| #1 | Small Talk | [[what-so-what-now-what]] | Make it about them, not you (support vs. shift responses) |
| #2 | Toasts/Tributes/Intros | WHAT (Why-How-Anecdotes-Thank) | Think of it as a gift you give |
| #3 | Pitching | [[problem-solution-benefit]] | Imperfection invites collaboration |
| #4 | Q&A | ADD (Answer-Detail-Describe value) | Reframe Q&A as dialogue, not dodgeball |
| #5 | Feedback | 4 I's (Information-Impact-Invitation-Implications) | Frame as collaborative problem-solving |
| #6 | Apologies | AAA (Acknowledge-Appreciate-Amends) | Apologizing is strength, not weakness |
