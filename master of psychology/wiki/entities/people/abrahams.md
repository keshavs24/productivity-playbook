# Matt Abrahams

**Type:** Person
**Phase(s):** 7
**Key Contribution:** Developer of the Think Faster, Talk Smarter methodology for spontaneous communication; Stanford GSB lecturer; host of the *Think Fast, Talk Smart* podcast

## Overview

Matt Abrahams is a lecturer in Organizational Behavior at Stanford University's Graduate School of Business (GSB) and the Stanford Continuing Studies program. He is the author of *Think Faster, Talk Smarter: How to Speak Successfully When You're Put on the Spot* (2023) and the earlier *Speaking Up Without Freaking Out* (2016). He hosts the *Think Fast, Talk Smart: The Podcast*, one of the most popular communication-focused podcasts.

Abrahams' core insight is that [[spontaneous-speaking]] -- the unplanned, in-the-moment communication that constitutes the vast majority of our interactions -- is a learnable skill that can be systematically improved through practice, mindset shifts, and internalized communication structures. His six-step methodology (Calm, Unlock, Redefine, Listen, Structure, Focus) draws on research from communication science, psychology, evolutionary biology, sociology, education, and improvisational theater.

His personal background uniquely prepared him for this work: with a last name starting with "Ab," he was almost always called on first in class, giving him decades of enforced practice with spontaneous speaking. He also taught high school English, worked as a Stanford campus tour guide, and has trained extensively in martial arts -- all experiences that inform his approach to performing under pressure.

Abrahams co-teaches the "Improvisationally Speaking" class at Stanford with improv expert Adam Tobin, blending academic research with practical improv techniques. His methodology has been adopted by Stanford MBA students, corporate clients, entrepreneurs, nonprofits, and government groups.
